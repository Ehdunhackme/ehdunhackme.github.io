About Me

I'm currently building across AI safety evaluation in LLMs, mechanistic interpretability, and AI4Astro. My research interest lies in understanding how models work internally (layer-wise) and how this information can advance our progress in AI, while making sure they're safe to deploy in real-world settings.

Before that, I represented Malaysia at the International Olympiad on Astronomy and Astrophysics (IOAA) 2025 and the International Astronomy Olympiad (IAO) 2025; I also founded MYResearchGuide, a non-profit initiative to democratise science research opportunities for Malaysian youth.
