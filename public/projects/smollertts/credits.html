SmollerTTS pairs our acoustic model with the pretrained HiFi-GAN LJ_V2 vocoder. These are the papers, data and tools behind it.

Papers & data

  • HiFi-GAN: Generative Adversarial Networks for Efficient and High Fidelity Speech Synthesis

    Jungil Kong, Jaehyeon Kim and Jaekyoung Bae. NeurIPS, 2020.

    Our waveform generator uses the published LJ_V2 weights.

  • FastSpeech 2: Fast and High-Quality End-to-End Text to Speech

    Yi Ren et al. ICLR, 2021.

    Architectural reference for duration, pitch and energy conditioning.

  • Montreal Forced Aligner: Trainable Text-Speech Alignment Using Kaldi

    Michael McAuliffe et al. Interspeech, 2017.

    Alignment tools used to prepare training targets.

  • UTMOS: UTokyo-SaruLab System for VoiceMOS Challenge 2022

    Takaaki Saeki et al. Interspeech, 2022.

    The automatic quality estimator used in the comparison chart.

  • The LJ Speech Dataset

    The English recordings used to train our acoustic model.

Software & sources

ONNX Runtime runs inference in the browser. Pronunciation uses MFA dictionaries and phonemize; number reading uses to-words.

Published comparison results come from tts-bench. Our separate 48-sentence test compares SmollerTTS with Tiny Voca, using UTMOS and faster-whisper. The interface uses the Geist typeface.
